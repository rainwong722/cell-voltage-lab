    Teacher notes

    Readings follow Table 29.1 of the textbook (Cu as reference on the red probe): Mg/Cu +1.84 V, Zn/Cu +0.92 V, Fe/Cu +0.49 V, Cu/Cu 0.00 V, Ag/Cu −0.17 V. Pb (+0.32 V) and Metal X (+1.30 V vs Cu) are simulated values for extension. Every reading equals the difference between the two metals' values, so Mission 3 predictions add up exactly (e.g. Zn/Ag = 0.92 + 0.17 = 1.09 V).

    Electron flow and the E.C.S. are hidden at the start so students build the rule from their own data first. Suggested order: Mission 1 → reveal the E.C.S. → Mission 2 → reveal electron flow → Missions 3–4.

    Terms: in a chemical cell the positive electrode is the cathode (reduction, gains electrons) and the negative electrode is the anode (oxidation, loses electrons). A digital multimeter shows a positive reading when the red (VΩ) terminal is connected to the positive electrode.
